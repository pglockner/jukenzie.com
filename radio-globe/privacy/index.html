Privacy policy

Radio Globe, for Android and iPhone

Radio Globe collects no data about you. Jukenzie runs no servers for the app, so nothing you do in it reaches Jukenzie unless you choose to send feedback.

What the app doesn't do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It has no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos.
  • It doesn't record, download or pass on the radio it plays.

What playing radio involves

To find and play stations, the app connects from your phone straight to other people's servers. As with any website you visit, each of them sees your IP address and may keep its own logs. Jukenzie doesn't run them and can't see those connections.

  • The station directory. Station lists come from Radio Browser, a community directory. The app sends it what you ask for: the words you search for, and the country, genre or format you open or turn the globe to. When you start a station, the app tells the directory that station was played, which is how the directory ranks stations for everyone. These requests carry the app's name and version and nothing that identifies you.
  • The stations. Sound comes directly from each broadcaster's own stream, and a station's logo from the address the station lists for it. The broadcaster sees your IP address and that a player connected, as it would for any listener.
  • Links you open. A station's website, and a search for the song playing in Apple Music or YouTube Music, open outside the app, in your browser or that service's app, under their own privacy policies.

What stays on your phone

Radio Globe saves a few things on the phone: your favorites, your recent searches, the last station you played, your filter and display choices, and a copy of the station list so the app opens quickly. They never leave the phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

Sending feedback

The app keeps a short diagnostic log while it runs: which stations were played, their stream addresses, and what the player did. The app doesn't save it to a file, and it is gone when the app closes; like any app's messages, its lines also pass through the phone's own system log, which stays on the phone. The log leaves your phone only if you open Feedback and send it. The Feedback screen shows everything that will be sent first: the app's version, your phone's model and system version, and the log. It then opens your phone's share sheet, and the text goes only where you choose to send it.

If you write to the support address, your message and email address are used only to answer you.

Children

Radio Globe collects nothing, so it collects nothing from children either. The stations are public broadcasts from around the world that Jukenzie doesn't choose or review, so what they play is up to each broadcaster.

Changes and contact
