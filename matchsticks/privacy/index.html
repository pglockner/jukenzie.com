Privacy policy

Matchsticks, for iPhone and iPad

Matchsticks collects no data about you. Nothing you do in the app is sent to Jukenzie or to anyone else.

What the app doesn't do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It has no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • It makes no network connections; it works the same with no internet at all.

What stays on your device

Matchsticks saves a few settings on your iPhone or iPad, such as whether music, sound and haptics are on, and how the screen is laid out. They never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing a puzzle

When you copy or share a puzzle's link, it goes through the iOS share sheet to the app you choose. The link holds only the puzzle's seed, such as hard-8f3a2c. Matchsticks doesn't see what you send or to whom.

Children

Matchsticks is suitable for all ages, and since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: support@jukenzie.com.
