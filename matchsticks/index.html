Matchsticks

Move a stick, make the shape

The old matchstick puzzle, made for iPhone and iPad. Each board is a figure of sticks and a goal: so many squares, or so many triangles. Pick up a stick, drop it into a free spot, and reach the goal within the moves you have.

  • A seven-round tutorial, then endless Easy, Medium and Hard puzzles
  • Every endless puzzle has a seed you can share, so a friend gets exactly your board
  • A two-step hint when you're stuck
  • No ads, no account, no internet connection needed

Get it on the App Store

Free, for iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to support@jukenzie.com. It helps to say which iPhone or iPad you have, and the app's version, shown at the foot of its menu.

Stuck on a puzzle? The question-mark button on every board explains the goal's symbols and buttons, and its lightbulb gives a hint.
